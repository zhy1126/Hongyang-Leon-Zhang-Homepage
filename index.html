About Me

I pursue two related streams of research: how AI can support social science research, and how it shapes human judgment and decision-making in legal settings. My work combines empirical studies with approaches from computational social science and human-computer interaction (HCI).

I hold an MPhil in Criminology from the University of Cambridge and an LL.B. from Zhongnan University of Economics and Law.

I welcome conversations about criminology, empirical legal research, and human-centered AI.

Research Interests

How do places, urban environments, and social contexts shape crime and safety?

I connect criminological theory with spatial and causal evidence to study when environments prevent, concentrate, or displace harm.
